Background

Knee Imaging Background

2D × 2 → 3D × 4

Two complementary tools

Radiographs are accessible but collapse depth. CT resolves 3D anatomy but adds cost and radiation. This project reconstructs femur, tibia, patella and fibula from AP and lateral views.

Conclusion

Conclusion and Contributions

.455 / 11.44 mm

Residual–ReLU performed best

O1–O4 were completed through an auditable cohort, controlled comparison and failure-aware evaluation. The computing contribution is evidence about decoder topology—not a CT replacement or clinically ready system.

What the evidence supports

  • Residual topology was the decisive factor.
  • Small bones remain the main bottleneck.
  • Clinical fracture preservation remains unproven.
